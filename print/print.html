How the pacer works

1 · Track

World Athletics geometry: 36.50 m kerb, 84.39 m straights, lanes 1–8; lane 1 measured 0.30 m out (400.00 m). Placed by take-off point, offset or GPS.

2 · Schedule

A virtual runner's s(t), v(t), a(t): run-up to pace before the line, per-lap targets, pace changes that still land every lap on time.

3 · Setpoints

50 Hz on PX4's clock: position, tangent velocity, turn + drag acceleration, nose on the tangent, via MAVSDK offboard.

4 · Scoring

From the ULog: line crossings at 50 Hz, gap to the runner, off-line on straights, bends and joins, height, lap-to-lap spread.

What the logs showed (findings 1–3, 5 on PX4 SIH; 4 on Gazebo)

1

Position-only setpoints look right and run late. Laps came in within ms of 59 s, yet the drone crossed the line s late every lap, m behind the runner. PX4's position loop makes the drone trail by speed ÷ MPC_XY_P (6.78 / 0.95 ≈ 7.1 m). The pacer sends position, velocity and acceleration together at 50 Hz.

2

A waypoint mission can't hold pace through bends. 71 fly-through waypoints at 6.78 m/s with PX4 defaults gave laps of against 59 s. Bends averaged m/s. Missions have no time target, so pacing needs the setpoint stream.

3

Drag goes in the feed-forward, measured from a log. Without it the drone ran m wide on bends and lost up to ms a lap. A least-squares fit on any flight log gives the coefficient: 1.008 from the SIH estimate (true 1.00), and for the Gazebo x500 from one calibration lap.

4

A GPS-delay mismatch hides inside perfect laps. In the first Gazebo run every lap was within ms, yet every crossing was ms late. PX4's default EKF2_GPS_DELAY (110 ms) against Gazebo's zero-latency GPS put the estimate m ahead of the drone. Matched to 0, the mean crossing became ms. On hardware it must match the receiver.

5

Time comes from the autopilot. Setpoints are computed for PX4's own clock; worst gap ms against the 1000 ms offboard-loss failsafe; offboard never dropped.

Phase 1, and what's already working beyond it

Phase 1 scope

  • PX4 SITL with Gazebo Harmonic (x500) and PX4's built-in SIH quadcopter
  • Gazebo world with a standard 400 m track, 8 lanes, World Athletics dimensions
  • Oval flight path: two 84.39 m straights, two 36.80 m-radius bends, continuous
  • Repeatable laps: m mean lap-to-lap spread in Gazebo
  • Source, config files, setup steps for Ubuntu 22.04 / 24.04, 51 tests

Already prototyped for later phases

  • Programmable lap times, per lap: 72-66-62-59-56 s flown within ± ms
  • Constant pace through bends; flying beside a runner with corrected bend speed
  • Timing and repeatability report from every flight log
  • Pace planner: enter splits, check PX4 limits, export the config
  • Drag identification for a new airframe from one flight
